<div><img src="/assets/images/about/profile.png" alt="Lucian Mocan" class="profile-float"></div>

I'm **Lucian**, a systems researcher and software engineer pursuing my M.S. in Computer Science, Networks, Internet and Systems Engineering at the [University of Strasbourg](https://mathinfo.unistra.fr/formations/master/master-informatique/odf-parcours-science-et-ingenierie-des-reseaux-de-linternet-et-des-systemes-siris-PR12-18105/?tab=cours) (B.S., 6th out of 122), alongside the selective five-year [Cursus Master en Ingénierie](https://mathinfo.unistra.fr/en/courses/masters-degree-of-engineering-in-computer-science/odf-subprogram-cursus-master-ingenierie-cmi-informatique-systemes-et-reseaux-PR1233-18114/) (CMI) track. I am drawn to foundational software: tools, runtimes, languages, and infrastructure that other engineers, researchers, and scientists can build on.

Currently, I am a visiting student researcher at [Northwestern University](https://www.mccormick.northwestern.edu/)'s [Prescience Lab](http://www.presciencelab.org/) under Professor [Peter Dinda](http://pdinda.org/), where I work on systems research and explore how AI can accelerate, support, and potentially automate parts of that work. This work became my master's thesis, *Medovik: Simplifying Porting to Trusted Execution Environments*.

Previously, advised by Professor [Quentin Bramas](https://bramas.fr/), I contributed to [Althread](https://althread.github.io/en/docs/guide/intro/), an educational language for model checking distributed systems, extending [its Rust compiler and import system](/projects/althread_import/) and building a [web IDE](https://althread.github.io/editor/). I've also applied that same systems thinking outside the lab: at [Emerson](https://www.emerson.com/en/final-control), I built a [full-stack platform](/projects/emerson_internship/) that saved over 800 hours annually, and I led a student team designing [a privacy-centric IoT platform](/projects/sharing_square/) for crowdsourcing scientific data.

I am actively seeking internship, job, and research collaboration opportunities starting in September 2026, especially roles such as systems engineer, software engineer, research engineer, or developer tooling engineer, working on open-source developer tools, agentic tooling, evaluation harnesses, command-line interfaces, and infrastructure for research and engineering workflows. I am also open to adjacent roles where I can build useful systems and learn quickly.
